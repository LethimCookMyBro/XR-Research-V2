# S1 Reference information

The task information a participant may open during S1 tasks. The participant decides when to open it and which format to read.

## Language

### Tasks

**Computer assembly (Task A)**:
Choosing RAM, SSD and GPU that meet W2 and fitting them to a prepared computer base. Nothing installed is faulty.
_Avoid_: component replacement, PC repair

**Fan repair (Task B)**:
Finding which of three areas of fan F2 (front guard, blade, rear guard) is loose or not seated, correcting it and reassembling.
_Avoid_: fan maintenance, fault finding

**Area**:
One of the three places in Fan repair where a fault could be: front guard, blade, rear guard. Information treats all three with equal weight and never reveals which one is faulty in a trial.
_Avoid_: fault point, broken part

**Answer leak**:
Anything in information that tells a participant which Task A part ID to choose or which Task B area is faulty, including by colour, emphasis, order, camera framing or a visible label in an assembly demonstration.
_Avoid_: hint, spoiler

**Demonstration part**:
A part shown being handled in Video or Illustrated whose identity cannot be read, so the motion is clear without an answer leak.
_Avoid_: example part, dummy

### Information

**Information format**:
One of four presentations of the same task information: Manual (PDF), Step-by-step, Illustrated and Video. Each format has its own job (see below); all four carry every essential unit.
_Avoid_: 4 information, media type, mode

**Essential unit**:
One fact a participant needs to complete the task correctly, such as "RAM goes in A2 and B2" or "the spinner loosens clockwise from the front". Every information format must carry every essential unit. The set of essential units changes only by a spec amendment, never by editing content.
_Avoid_: key point, requirement (reserved for W2 values)

**Manual (PDF)**:
The format for looking things up: contents and the W2 requirement table first, then one reference page per section with normal state, specifications, check points, how to fit or remove, and cautions. Option label values sit on their section page. It does not repeat Step-by-step's procedures.
_Avoid_: document, reference page

**Step-by-step**:
The format for doing: one text card per job, naming the action, the control and what done looks like. It opens on a contents card that jumps to any job and suggests no single required order. In Fan repair each area has check and correct cards, as many as that area needs.
_Avoid_: checklist, instructions

**Illustrated**:
The format for locating: one picture of the real model per job, with arrows for where and which direction, and as little text as possible.
_Avoid_: images, diagram

**Video**:
The format for seeing motion: recorded clips of the real model being grasped, inserted, removed and turned, showing the result of each action, with Thai or Japanese narration.
_Avoid_: slideshow, animation

**Contents**:
The first view of every information format, listing the same main topics under the same job names. Formats may differ in how many pages, cards, pictures or chapters sit under each topic.
_Avoid_: menu, index page

**Chapter**:
One job within the Video, such as "fit RAM" or "remove the front guard". The participant opens any chapter in any order. Its length follows the action, not a fixed limit.
_Avoid_: scene, segment

**Job**:
One self-contained piece of work that ends in a visible result, such as seating a GPU and tightening its bracket screw. Step-by-step cards, Illustrated pictures and Video chapters are each one job.
_Avoid_: step (ambiguous with single hand movements), action
